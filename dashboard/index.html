A data-led watchlist of sizeable protocols with no recorded token. The model ranks estimated value; it does not predict a launch.

How the value is worked out

Implied value = 8% of TVL × 7d-growth factor × token-less tenure factor × chain breadth × category-only launch-plausibility factor. Growth is capped at ±50%; tenure uses listed_at through the snapshot date. CEX and bridge categories get ×0.003; liquid staking and restaked BTC get ×0.02, because these are not farmable pre-token launches. Liquid restaking gets ×0.55; farmable DeFi categories receive ×1.05.
